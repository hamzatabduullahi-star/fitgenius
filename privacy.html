Privacy Policy

Understand how FitGenius handles your information and what privacy means when using our platform.

Last updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to FitGenius. We value your privacy and believe you should understand how information associated with your account is handled.

This policy explains the types of information that may be processed when you use FitGenius, why it is needed and how you can contact us about your privacy.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, FitGenius may process the following information:

  • Account information such as your name and email address.
  • Age information if you choose to provide it in your profile.
  • Account preferences and notification settings.
  • Fitness and wellness information when supported tracking features become available and you choose to use them.

We do not claim to collect health information from Apple Health or Android Health Connect through the current website.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage your FitGenius account.
  • Allow you to sign in and access your account.
  • Display profile information and preferences.
  • Improve the functionality and experience of the platform.
  • Support fitness and wellness features as they become available.

4. Data Storage and Security

FitGenius uses Supabase services for account authentication and related backend functionality.

We aim to protect information through appropriate technical and organisational safeguards. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

You should keep your password private and avoid sharing your account credentials with others.

5. Sharing of Information

FitGenius does not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that help operate the platform, such as the authentication and hosting services we use.

Such providers may process information as necessary to provide their services and subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

FitGenius may use browser storage technologies such as local storage to remember certain preferences or settings.

You can manage or clear locally stored information through your browser settings. Doing so may affect some website features.

7. Your Privacy Choices

You can review and update profile details through the account settings available on the platform, where those features are supported.

You may also contact FitGenius to ask questions about your information or request assistance with account-related privacy concerns.

8. Children's Privacy

FitGenius is not intended to be used by children below the minimum age permitted by applicable law. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy requirements.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as FitGenius develops new features or changes how the platform operates.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how FitGenius handles information, please contact the FitGenius team.

An official support email address will be provided here when one is established.
