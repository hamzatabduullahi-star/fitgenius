YOUR PRIVACY MATTERS

Privacy Policy

At FitGenius, we respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how information is handled when you use our platform.

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, FitGenius may process information such as:

  • Your name and email address.
  • Profile information you choose to provide.
  • Fitness activity information such as steps and distance.
  • Sleep and recovery information, if you connect a supported device or health service.
  • Your notification and application preferences.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information may be used to:

  • Personalise your FitGenius experience.
  • Display your fitness and wellness information.
  • Provide activity summaries and wellness recommendations.
  • Improve platform performance and functionality.
  • Support features you choose to activate.

3. Health and Fitness Information

FitGenius is designed to support fitness awareness and general wellness. Health and fitness information may be accessed only when the relevant integrations are implemented and you grant the required permissions.

You should review the permissions requested by your device and connected health services before enabling any integration.

4. Local Storage

Some current FitGenius settings and profile details may be stored locally in your browser using localStorage. This information remains on the browser where it was saved unless it is cleared or the application is changed.

5. Information Sharing

FitGenius does not currently provide a feature for selling your personal information. If future services require third-party providers, we will explain the relevant data sharing and purposes before those services are introduced.

6. Data Security

We aim to use appropriate safeguards to protect information handled by FitGenius. However, no online platform or storage method can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices

You can:

  • Choose what profile information you provide.
  • Change your saved preferences.
  • Manage device permissions for connected services.
  • Clear locally stored browser information.

8. Children's Privacy

FitGenius is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly design the platform to collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as FitGenius develops. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the FitGenius team through the contact information provided on our website.
